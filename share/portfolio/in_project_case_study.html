Important Findings in the Demonstration

  • Contingency burn ratio was the strongest tested early-warning relationship with forecast overrun.
  • RFI response time was more informative than raw RFI volume for schedule-delay analysis.
  • Calendar completion variance remained necessary even when portfolio SPI approached 1.00.
  • Project-type and delivery-method comparisons were treated as portfolio observations—not industry benchmarks.
